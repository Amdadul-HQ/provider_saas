# REQ-001 Core ERP
As a provider I can: sign up, create my business (tenant), invite staff with roles; manage customers (name, phones, social handles, tags, notes, due balance); manage products with plans (duration, price, cost); create subscriptions (start/expiry, price, slot), see expiring list; issue invoices, record payments (bKash/Nagad/Rocket/bank/cash) with TrxID, partial payments and due ledger; see dashboard numbers (MRR, active subs, expiring in 7 days, dues, profit this month).
Acceptance: all above via GraphQL + UI; tenant isolation tested.
